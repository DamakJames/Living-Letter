## 1. FOUNDER PROFILE

### Pastor Solomon Amao
**Founder & Lead Teacher**

Living Letters was founded by Pastor Solomon Amao with an urgent and focused mandate: to raise believers who are firmly rooted in sound doctrine, sensitive to the Holy Spirit, and actively expressive of Jesus Christ in the rhythm of daily life.

### Ministry Commitment
Living Letters is unreservedly committed to:
- **Sound Doctrine:** Uncompromising biblical fidelity and Christocentric hermeneutics.
- **Spiritual Growth:** Moving believers from infancy to grounded spiritual maturity.
- **Practical Living:** Translating deep theological truths into workplace, home, and community lifestyle.

## 2. MINISTRY VISION & SCRIPTURAL INSPIRATION

### The Scripture Anchor
> *"You yourselves are our letter of recommendation, written on our hearts, to be known and read by all. And you show that you are a letter from Christ delivered by us, written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of human hearts."*  
> — **2 Corinthians 3:2–3 (ESV)**

### The Vision
Believers known and read by all men as God's authentic message and ambassadors to an unsettled world.

### The Mission
To equip every believer to:
1. **Replicate** the character, love, and works of Christ through the empowering presence of the Holy Spirit.
2. **Rule** in righteousness and spiritual discernment through the Gospel of the Son.
3. **Reflect** the wisdom, holiness, and glory of the Father in every sphere of influence.

---

## 3. WHAT WE BELIEVE (CORE DOCTRINE)

1. **The Sufficiency and Authority of Scripture:** The Holy Bible is God's inspired, inerrant, and fully sufficient guide for faith, doctrine, and holy living.
2. **The Centrality of Jesus Christ:** Jesus is the eternal Son of God, crucified for our transgressions, bodily resurrected for our justification, and reigning as Lord.
3. **The Ministry of the Holy Spirit:** The Spirit indwells, convicts, sanctifies, and empowers believers with spiritual gifts to walk in supernatural wisdom and obedience.
4. **Grace-Based Discipleship:** Salvation and Christian growth are by sovereign grace through faith, working out in demonstrable transformation and good works.
5. **Living Faith:** Christianity is not an intellectual ritual, but a living union with Christ that actively influences culture, family, and relationships.

---

## 4. OUR FIVE-POINT DISCIPLESHIP APPROACH

1. **Scripture-Centered:** Every sermon, study guide, and article is anchored directly in God’s written Word.
2. **Spirit-Led:** We lean continually on the Holy Spirit's illumination and transformative power.
3. **Practically Applied:** Clear exposition paired with practical action steps for modern everyday life.
4. **Community Minded:** Strengthening local bodies and equipping believers to disciple one another.
5. **Freely Shared:** Open-access digital media ensuring that anyone hungry for truth can access gospel discipleship without economic barriers.
