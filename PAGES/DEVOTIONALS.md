## 1. DEVOTIONAL PHILOSOPHY

Living Letters Devotionals are engineered to help believers pause, meditate on Scripture, and cultivate daily sensitivity to the Holy Spirit. Rather than shallow motivational thoughts, each reflection is anchored in expository biblical truth.

---

## 2. DEVOTIONAL FORMAT & ARCHITECTURE

Every devotional entry follows a clean 5-step structure:
1. **Title & Theme:** Focused, actionable heading.
2. **Anchor Scripture:** Complete verse text with biblical citation.
3. **Exposition & Reflection:** 300–500 words of crisp, scriptural insight.
4. **Key Takeaway / Truth to Remember:** One memorable sentence to carry throughout the day.
5. **Prayer & Action Step:** Guided prayer or specific behavioral response.
