## 3. "WHO WE ARE" (SCRIPTURE & SPIRIT)

- **Eyebrow:** Who We Are
- **Headline:** **A Ministry Rooted in Scripture & Spirit**
- **Body Copy:**  
  The Living Letters is a Christ-centered teaching ministry founded by **Pastor Solomon Amao**, focused on raising believers who understand Scripture, grow in spiritual maturity, and live out the life of Christ daily.
  
  The entire name and concept of *"Living Letters"* came when he read and meditated upon **2 Corinthians 3:1–18**, recognizing that God calls His people to be living, breathing epistles delivered by Christ to the world.
