# ❓ HEART’S QUESTIONS (Q&A VIDEO SERIES SPECIFICATION)

**Series Title:** Heart’s Questions  
**Lead Teacher:** Pastor Solomon Amao  
**Format:** Long-Form Expository Video Teaching (2 Episodes / Week)  
**Release Schedule:** Every Tuesday & Friday  
**Status:** Active Production

---

## 1. VISION & PURPOSE

"Heart’s Questions" is an intentional discipleship video broadcast where Pastor Solomon Amao addresses honest, deep-seated questions submitted by believers and seekers.

Unlike short bite-sized clips, each episode is a **25–40 minute long-form teaching** that provides:
- Exegesis of the primary biblical passages
- Contextual understanding of human struggle and culture
- Practical, Spirit-led application
- Guided prayer and pastoral counsel

---

## 2. WEEKLY PRODUCTION SCHEDULE

| Day | Release Type | Focus Area | Standard Duration |

## 3. CORE EPISODES IN CATALOG

### Episode 1: How Do I Discern God’s Voice When Making Major Life Decisions?
- **Schedule:** Tuesday Release
- **Scriptures:** John 10:27, Proverbs 3:5–6, Romans 12:1–2, Colossians 3:15
- **Duration:** 28:45
- **Key Focus:** The harmony between the written Word, the inward witness of peace, and mature godly counsel.

### Episode 2: Overcoming Lingering Guilt and Condemnation After Repentance
- **Schedule:** Friday Release
- **Scriptures:** Romans 8:1, 1 John 1:9, Hebrews 10:14–17
- **Duration:** 32:10
- **Key Focus:** Silencing the accuser of the brethren with the finished redemption of Christ. Differentiating Holy Spirit conviction from demonic condemnation.

### Episode 3: What Is the True Biblical Purpose of Fasting and Prayer?
- **Schedule:** Tuesday Release
- **Scriptures:** Matthew 6:16–18, Isaiah 58:6–9, Acts 13:2–3
- **Duration:** 26:15
- **Key Focus:** Fasting as physical subjugation and spiritual tuning, rather than earning God's favor.

### Episode 4: How Can I Grow Spiritually When Facing Continual Obstacles?
- **Schedule:** Friday Release
- **Scriptures:** James 1:2–4, Romans 5:3–5, 2 Peter 3:18
- **Duration:** 35:40
- **Key Focus:** Trials as the crucible where theoretical theology matures into experiential spiritual authority.
