## 1. PURPOSE & AUDIENCE

The Living Letters Podcast delivers expository, Christ-centered audio teachings designed to strengthen faith, deepen theological discernment, and train believers in Spirit-led living.

---

## 2. EPISODE SPECIFICATIONS

Each podcast episode includes:
1. **Full High-Quality Audio Stream:** Clean stream with scrubbable progress bar, playback speed options (1x, 1.25x, 1.5x), and timestamps.
2. **Episode Metadata:** Title, speaker, series tag, duration, release date, and biblical references.
3. **Exposition & Key Insights:** Bulleted summary of core principles and doctrinal takeaways.
4. **Show Notes & Scripture Index:** Comprehensive list of scriptures read during the sermon.
5. **Download Options:** Direct MP3 download for offline listening in regions with low connectivity.
6. **Syndication Links:** Direct buttons for Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube Music, and RSS feed.
