## USER JOURNEYS

### Journey 1: New Visitor
1. Land on Homepage → See Hero + Mission
2. Click "Explore Teachings" → Browse content library
3. Subscribe to email → Get into pipeline

### Journey 2: Returning Listener
1. Go to Podcast → Find latest message
2. Listen + read show notes
3. Explore related series

### Journey 3: Deep Learner
1. Browse series page
2. Commit to multi-part series
3. Complete & share insights
