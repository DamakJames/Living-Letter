Who We Are

A Ministry Rooted in Scripture & Spirit

The Living Letters is a Christ-centered teaching ministry founded by The Living Letters, focused on raising believers who understand Scripture, grow in spiritual maturity, and live out the life of Christ daily.

The entire name and concept of "Living Letters" came when he read and meditated upon 2 Corinthians 3:1–18, realizing that God calls His people to be living, breathing epistles delivered by Christ to the world.

"You show that you are a letter from Christ delivered by us, written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of human hearts." 2 Corinthians 3:2–3
