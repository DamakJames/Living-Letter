## DECISION NEEDED NOW: WHICH PATH?

### Path A: DIY WordPress
**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks
**Cost:** $100-300/year hosting + theme
**Complexity:** Medium (requires technical help)
**Best for:** Maximum control, future growth

### Path B: Webflow
**Timeline:** 3-4 weeks
**Cost:** $150-300/month
**Complexity:** Low-Medium (visual builder)
**Best for:** Beautiful design quickly, minimal maintenance

### Path C: Hire a Developer
**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks
**Cost:** $2,000-10,000+
**Complexity:** Handled by professional
**Best for:** Premium, custom solution
